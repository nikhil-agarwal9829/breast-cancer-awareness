Prevention & Early Detection

Regular Screening

  • Annual mammograms starting at age 40 (or earlier if high risk)
  • Clinical breast exams every 1-3 years (ages 20-39)
  • Monthly self-breast examinations
  • MRI screening for high-risk individuals
  • Genetic counseling if family history exists

Healthy Lifestyle

  • Maintain a healthy weight (BMI 18.5-24.9)
  • Eat a balanced diet rich in fruits and vegetables
  • Limit alcohol consumption (max 1 drink/day)
  • Exercise regularly (150 minutes/week moderate activity)
  • Avoid smoking and secondhand smoke

Risk Reduction

  • Breastfeed if possible (reduces risk)
  • Limit hormone replacement therapy duration
  • Consider preventive medications if high risk
  • Manage stress and get adequate sleep
  • Stay informed about your family history

Early Detection Signs

  • New lump or mass in breast or underarm
  • Changes in breast size or shape
  • Skin dimpling or puckering
  • Nipple discharge (especially bloody)
  • Persistent breast or nipple pain

Support & Education

  • Join support groups for emotional support
  • Educate yourself about breast health
  • Talk to your doctor about your risk factors
  • Share family medical history with healthcare providers
  • Stay up-to-date with latest research and guidelines

Screening Schedule

  • 20-39: Clinical exam every 1-3 years
  • 40-49: Annual mammogram + clinical exam
  • 50+: Annual mammogram + clinical exam
  • High Risk: May need earlier/more frequent screening
  • Monthly self-exams recommended for all ages
